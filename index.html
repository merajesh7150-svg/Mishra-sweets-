Who We Are

A mithai counter & fast-food kitchen, side by side

Mishra Sweets is a warm, family-run spot in Nandgram where a traditional Indian sweets counter sits right alongside a full vegetarian fast-food kitchen. Guests come for our signature Milk Cake and Kalakand — and stay for quick bites and small plates too.

Whether it's a box of mithai for a celebration or a casual plate of fast food with the family, everything is prepared fresh, 100% vegetarian, and served with dine-in, takeaway or delivery.
